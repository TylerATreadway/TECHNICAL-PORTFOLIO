Important: Age eligibility alone does not establish entitlement. You must have sufficient covered work credits (usually 40) and apply for benefits. The chart estimates your own retirement benefit, not spousal, survivor or disability benefits. Claiming before FRA while working may cause payments to be withheld under the earnings test. Benefits are generally paid the month after the month they are due. Amounts exclude future cost-of-living adjustments, earnings changes, taxes, Medicare premiums, and some timing details for delayed retirement credits.
